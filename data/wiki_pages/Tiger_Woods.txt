Eldrick Tont "Tiger" Woods (born December 30, 1975) is an American professional golfer. Woods is widely regarded as one of the greatest golfers of all time and as one of the most famous athletes in modern history. He is tied for first in PGA Tour wins, ranks second in men's major championships, holds numerous golf records, and is an inductee of the World Golf Hall of Fame.
Following an outstanding junior, college, and amateur career, Woods turned professional in 1996 at age 20. By April 1997, he had won three PGA Tour events and his first major, the 1997 Masters, which he won by 12 strokes in a record-setting performance.
