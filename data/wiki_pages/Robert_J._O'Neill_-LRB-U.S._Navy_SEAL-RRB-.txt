Robert J. O'Neill (born 10 April 1976) is a former United States Navy SEAL (1996–2012), TV news contributor, and author.  After participating in May 2011's Operation Neptune Spear with SEAL Team Six, O'Neill was the subject of controversy for claiming to be the sole individual to kill Osama bin Laden.


== Personal life ==
Born in Butte, Montana, on 10 April 1976, Robert J. O'Neill is the son of Tom O'Neill, Jim Johnson, and Diane Johnson.  He graduated from Butte Central High School in 1994, and has attended Montana Technological University.  O'Neill married in March 2004, and has at least two children; he and his spouse were legally separated by February 2013.
