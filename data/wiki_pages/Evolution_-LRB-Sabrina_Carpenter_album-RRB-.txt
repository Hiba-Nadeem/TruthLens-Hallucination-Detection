Evolution is the second studio album by American singer Sabrina Carpenter, released on October 14, 2016, by Hollywood Records. Carpenter began recording the album in 2015, shortly after the launch of her first project, Eyes Wide Open until 2016. Musically, Evolution is a dance-pop record, which departs from the folk-pop and teen pop sound of its predecessor. Evolution received positive reviews from music critics and reached number 28 on the US Billboard 200 chart.


== Promotion ==
"Smoke and Fire" was originally intended to be the lead single from Evolution, but it was eventually excluded from the track listing; Carpenter later said that this was due to the "evolution" occurring after the song.
