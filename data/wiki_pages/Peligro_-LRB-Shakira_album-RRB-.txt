Peligro (English: Danger, Spanish pronunciation: [peˈli.ɣ̞ɾo]) is the second studio album by Colombian singer-songwriter Shakira, released on 25 March 1993 by Sony Music Colombia. The third single, "Eres", earned her the third place at the 1993 Viña del Mar International Song Festival.


== Background ==
Shakira was signed to Sony Music Colombia at the age of 13, with the deal to record three albums. While Magia, her debut album, was not much of a success commercially, with only 1,200 copies sold, the songs from that record garnered frequent radio play on Colombian stations and, according to author Ximena Diego, showed her potential.
Despite Magia's low sales, Sony Colombia had greater expectations for Shakira's second album, hoping that it would heighten the limited popularity she was already enjoying.
