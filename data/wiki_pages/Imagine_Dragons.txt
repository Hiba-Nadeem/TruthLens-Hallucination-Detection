Imagine Dragons are an American pop rock band formed in 2008, based in Las Vegas, Nevada. The band currently consists of lead singer Dan Reynolds, guitarist Wayne Sermon, and bassist Ben McKee. Their debut single, "It's Time", was released in February 2012, reaching the Top 20 of various charts around the world. Their debut album, Night Visions, which resulted in the chart-topping singles "Radioactive" and "Demons", reached No 2 in America on release in September 2012, and achieved Platinum sales by June 2013. Rolling Stone named "Radioactive", which held the record for most weeks charted on the Billboard Hot 100, the "biggest rock hit of the year".
