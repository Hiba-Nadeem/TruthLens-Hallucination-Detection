Ramayanam, also known as Bala Ramayanam (transl. Child Ramayanam), is a 1997 Indian Telugu-language mythological film directed by Gunasekhar and produced by M. S. Reddy. Based on the Hindu epic Ramayana, the film features overs 3000 child actors. It stars N. T. Rama Rao Jr. as Lord Rama  Smitha Madhav as Sita Swathi Kodali as Ravanasura, won the NANDI AWARD for BEST CHILD ACTOR.
The film is released on 11 April 1997. It received the National Film Award for Best Children's Film and two Nandi Awards.
