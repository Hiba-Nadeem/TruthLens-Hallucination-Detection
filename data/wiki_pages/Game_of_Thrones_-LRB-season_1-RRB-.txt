The first season of the fantasy drama television series Game of Thrones premiered on HBO on April 17, 2011, in the United States and concluded on June 19, 2011. It consists of ten episodes of approximately 55 minutes each. The series is based on A Game of Thrones, the first novel in the A Song of Ice and Fire series by George R. R. Martin, adapted for television by David Benioff and D. B. Weiss. HBO had ordered a television pilot in November 2008; filming began the following year. However, it did not receive a season order and was later reworked with some roles recast.
